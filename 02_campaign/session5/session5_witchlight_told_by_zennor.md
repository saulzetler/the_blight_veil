# THE BLIGHT VEIL

## Session 5 — As Told by Zennor: The Witchlight

*Human. Fighter. Amulet: cold, same as it's been for years — until tonight.*

---

We had just come up out of the roots. The rest of that day belongs to the others — for most of it I was swinging a sword at something resembling Oscar. This part is mine.

We crossed a platform above the river, south side, past where the lanterns give out. I looked at the far bank. I don't know why.

She was standing at the waterline. Still. Too far for a face, but I know that light — low and patient, the color of something seen through a shut eyelid. The last thing I saw before I stopped being able to look at my wife.

Both hands at her sides. Watching me.

I blinked once. She was gone. No splash. No footsteps. The river didn't change its sound at all.

I went down to the bank. The mud was wet everywhere except one mark — a hand, pressed flat, fingers spread toward the water. The center of it was dry. Warm under my palm.

I know that light. I know she's close.

I haven't decided what to do about either.
