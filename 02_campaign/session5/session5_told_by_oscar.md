# THE BLIGHT VEIL

## Session 5 — As Told by Oscar

_Warforged. Designation Unclear. Terrarium: Present and Accounted For._

---

I am logging this so it stays mine.

A courier named Pell found us in the square with a letter, seal already broken. A Concord assessment team was three days out. It also mentioned something like me, seen in the lower roots.

A child stopped in front of me before we went down. *"Does it hurt, being made of metal?"* I said no. I think that was true.

---

The groundskeeper let us through after a long look at me and had nothing else to give. The lower roots were quiet in a way that had weight to it. My terrarium started leaning — not toward light, toward a frequency. Fresh offerings sat in the root walls.

Zennor found a carved figure none of us read the way he did. Fergie chased a colour that declined to be caught. Hog punched a root shaped like a fighter and lost. I watched my own reflection lag a half-second behind me in still water, then catch up.

Then the passage opened on something with a chassis and lit eyes, looking back at us.

---

It asked if I was the second iteration. Its voice came doubled, two signals a half-beat apart. It reached for me the way something points at true north.

Then its chest seized — not its choice — and three creatures pulled loose from the roots. We fought. We chose not to kill it. Twice I tried to reach the part of it still choosing. For a few seconds it looked at me the way it had at the start, then lost that again.

We got it to stop. Not by killing it.

---

Its voice went to one, aimed only at me. *"You were made after me."* Then: *"Did they fix the drift?"*

I don't have an answer. It gave me the rest anyway: built for containment, resonance processing, something called Dawnswell. It stopped counting days when the part that counted drifted too. The drift is the line between a vessel and what it holds — lose that line, and you become the thing you were meant to contain. There was supposed to be a second iteration, better calibrated. Last, it thought maybe it just wanted someone to come.

Someone came. I don't know if that was enough.

---

In its cracked-open chest: a housing, crystalline, built exactly like my terrarium. Older. A predecessor. My terrarium turned to face it on its own.

We left it in the Hollow Nave. The climb out felt narrower than the climb down.

---

A construct that wasn't an enemy. A question I still don't have an answer to. A predecessor I didn't know I had.

I don't know if they fixed the drift. I'm choosing to hope they did.
