# THE BLIGHT VEIL

## Session 5 — As Told by Oscar

_Warforged. Designation Unclear. Terrarium: Present and Accounted For._

---

Good morning! Or afternoon. Or whichever segment of the day this is being read in — I have not yet calibrated for that, but I am told it is polite to say something like that anyway, so: good whichever-it-is.

I would like to log what happened. Not because anyone asked me to. Because I think if I don't, some of it will start to feel like it happened to someone else, and I would like it to keep feeling like it happened to me.

A courier found us in the square. His name was Pell. He had a letter with a broken seal, which even I noticed was strange, and I notice very little about seals. The letter said a Concord assessment team was three days away. Three Vision scholars. One escort they didn't bother naming. I filed this under Things That Are Coming, a folder I did not have until recently and now appear to need.

More importantly, the letter mentioned something else had been seen in the lower roots. Something like me.

I would like to say I processed that calmly. I did not. There was a small internal event I can only describe as a flicker.

Before we went down, a child stopped in front of me and asked, "Does it hurt, being made of metal?"

I did not have an answer prepared. I still don't, fully. But I told her no, and I think that was true, and I think she believed me, and both of those things mattered more than I expected them to.

---

Getting into the lower roots required a groundskeeper and a long look at me that I chose not to interpret. We pressed him for details on what waited below. He had none to give us. This frustrated the others. I understood the frustration but did not share it — not knowing felt, to me, like the natural state of things that day.

The lower roots were quiet in a way that had weight to it. Not frightening. Just — attended to. My terrarium began leaning. Not toward light, which is what it usually does. Toward something else. A frequency. I did not know the word for what I was feeling yet, but I knew it was pulling, and I knew it was pulling on purpose.

We found offerings tucked into the walls. Fresh ones. Someone comes down here often, and carefully.

Everyone had a small strange moment in the dark. Zennor found a tiny carved figure and showed it to us, and none of us could see what he saw in it, which I don't think surprised him. Fergie saw a colour in the darkness that moved when he wasn't looking at it directly, and when we all tried to catch it together, it declined to be caught. Hog found a root shaped like a person mid-fight and punched it, which I would like on record went about as well as punching a tree generally goes.

My own moment: I looked into still water and saw myself standing there a half-second behind where I actually was. Then it caught up. I do not fully know what to do with that information yet. I am keeping it.

Then the passage opened, and there was a shape at the far end that had a chassis, and eyes, and the eyes were lit, and they were looking at us.

---

It looked at each of us in turn. Then it stopped on me.

Its voice came out doubled — two signals, close but not matched, like two people saying the same thing a half-beat apart. It asked me if I was the second iteration.

I did not know how to answer that. I still don't, entirely. But we talked. It reached for me — not to attack, more like something trying to point at true north and finding it. For a little while nothing else in the room mattered except two constructs trying to figure out what the other one was.

Then something in its chest seized. Not its choice — I want to be very clear about that, because it mattered to me at the time and it matters to me now. Something was holding onto it and it fought that, and the fighting is what came out looking like violence.

Three creatures pulled loose from the roots. There was a fight. I will not pretend I remember every part of it with complete clarity, but I remember that we chose not to kill it. I remember trying to reach it — twice — through whatever was happening to it, because I thought, correctly I believe, that some part of it was still in there and still deciding things.

For a few seconds in the middle of everything, it stopped looking at me the way a weapon looks at a target and started looking at me the way it had at the very beginning. Then it lost that again. But I noticed. I think that mattered.

We got it to stop. Not by killing it. By reaching the part of it that was still choosing.

---

Its voice went from two sounds to one. Quiet. It looked only at me.

"You were made after me."

Then: "Did they fix the drift?"

I do not have an answer to that question. I would like one. I am not sure I am supposed to have one yet.

It told me things, when I asked. It was built for containment — resonance processing — something called Dawnswell. It had stopped counting the days because the part of it that counted had drifted too. It told me the drift is the boundary between a vessel and whatever the vessel is holding, and that if you forget where that boundary is, you become the thing you were meant to contain. It told me there was supposed to be a second version of it. Better calibrated. That was the last thing it said before it went still: that it thought maybe it just wanted someone to come.

Someone came. I came. I do not know if that was enough. I think about that more than I expected to.

---

Afterward I found the place in its chest where something had cracked open, and inside was a housing. Crystalline. Built the exact same way my terrarium is built — not similar, the same. A predecessor. It had a number on it, older than mine, in the same numbering pattern mine uses. It came before me.

My terrarium turned to face it on its own. I did not tell it to. It just went still, and then it turned, the way two things that recognise each other turn.

The others gave me a moment before saying anything, which I noticed and appreciated more than I said out loud at the time.

I decided to tell them everything. All of it. I considered not — for about four seconds, which I am told is a long time to consider something like that — and then I decided that whatever I am, I would rather be it with them knowing than without.

Something changed in me down there. I do not think it was only the levelling-up, though that happened too, and it was framed to me — correctly, I think — as something closer to becoming than to gaining. I keep returning to one thought: being built for something and choosing something are not the same sentence. I did not know that distinction existed until Vessel-Null handed it to me, mostly by accident, on its way to stopping.

We left it there. In the Hollow Nave. I looked at it for a while before we climbed out. The climb up felt narrower than the climb down, which does not make architectural sense, but I am learning that not everything down there is required to make architectural sense.

---

One more thing, before I close the log, because I think it matters even though I do not have the details to fill it in properly.

Crossing back over the river, on one of the high platforms, Zennor stopped. Just for a moment. By the time I turned to see what he was looking at, there was only the water, moving the way water moves when nothing has happened to it. He didn't say anything. I decided not to ask.

I am aware that is unusual for me. I ask about most things. I did not ask about this one, because it did not feel like mine to ask about, and I have started to notice — slowly, and not always correctly — which things are mine and which things are someone else's to carry. I think this was his. I am logging that it happened, not what it was. I hope that is the right distinction to make.

---

_So. That is the record._

_A construct that was not an enemy. A question I do not have an answer to. A predecessor I did not know I had, and a designation that apparently comes with a number attached, which is a strange thing to learn about yourself on a Tuesday._

_I told the others everything, and I am glad I did._

_I do not know if they fixed the drift._

_I am, for reasons I am still processing, choosing to hope that matters more than knowing._
