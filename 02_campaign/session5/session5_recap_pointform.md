# The Blight Veil — Session 5 Recap (Point Form)

**Party:** Zennor (Kyle) · Hog Thunderwood (Dawson) · Fergie (Paul) · Oscar / 05C4R (Lo)

---

## The Messenger

- Pell the courier found the party in the central square and delivered a letter — seal already broken before it reached him
- The letter: a Concord Harmonic Assessment unit (three Vision scholars + one unlisted Might escort) is "three days out"
- Party asked Pell who sent the letter — he said he didn't know; he wasn't pressed further on the unlisted escort
- The letter surfaced the real lead: a construct like Oscar has been sighted in the lower roots
- A child stopped Oscar and asked: _"Does it hurt, being made of metal?"_

## Into the Lower Roots

- The groundskeeper let the party through after a long, silent look at Oscar
- Party pushed the groundskeeper for more information about what waited below — he had nothing to give, which frustrated the party
- The lower roots landed as uncanny, not hostile
- Oscar's terrarium began orienting — leaning ahead and down, toward a frequency rather than light
- Recent ritual offerings were found in root-wall hollows — soft wax, fresh flowers, deliberate cloth-knotting
- **Zennor** found a hollow with a child-sized carved figure; showed the party, but no one else saw anything different from the other carvings
- **Oscar** noticed their reflection in still water lagging a beat behind, then catching up normally
- **Fergie** spotted an ember-orange wisp moving in the dark between light sources; it dissipated when approached, and did nothing when the group tried to interact with it directly
- **Hog** found a section of root grown into an unmistakable fighting stance and punched it — hurt his fist on solid wood, and thoroughly confused everyone else
- The passage opened into the Hollow Nave — first sight of Vessel-Null, a shape with a chassis and lit eyes, not root and not stone

## The Hollow Nave — Vessel-Null

- Vessel-Null's eyes tracked the party and fixed on Oscar
- Its doubled voice (mechanical + resonance, out of sync) asked Oscar if he was "the second iteration"
- It reached toward Oscar like a compass finding north — the party had a full roleplay exchange before any combat began
- The crystalline growth in its chest spasmed — **the trigger was the growth acting, not Vessel-Null's will**
- Three Blighted Hollow Growths emerged from the root pillars
- Vessel-Null opened immediately with Resonance Pulse
- Blights used Root Grasp to restrain
- The party chose to subdue rather than kill
- Mid-fight, Vessel-Null stopped targeting Oscar for a few seconds — a moment of partial clarity — before snapping back
- The party tried to physically remove the crystalline growth; Vessel-Null: _"It's too far in."_
- Oscar attempted to interface with Vessel-Null, both during Phase 1 and during the later clarity window
- Vessel-Null reached the Clarity Window and was subdued — the doubled voice resolved to one quiet voice, looking only at Oscar
- Vessel-Null: _"You were made after me."_ · _"Did they fix the drift?"_ · _"I couldn't tell anymore what I was holding and what was holding me."_
- Subdued, it answered every question put to it:
  - _"What were you built for?"_ — "Containment. Resonance processing. They called it Dawnswell."
  - _"How long have you been down here?"_ — "I stopped counting. The counting mechanism drifted too."
  - _"What is the drift?"_ — "The boundary between the vessel and what the vessel holds."
  - _"Are there others like you?"_ — "There was supposed to be a second iteration. Better calibrated."
  - _"What do you want?"_ — "I think I just wanted someone to come."

## Oscar's Mirror

- Oscar found Vessel-Null's cracked-open chest cavity
- Inside: a crystalline housing — same principle, same orientation, same internal logic as Oscar's own terrarium. A predecessor.
- Vessel-Null's partial serial designation was legible — same prefix convention as Oscar's, lower numeral. Vessel-Null came first.
- Oscar's terrarium physically reoriented to face the housing — two resonance mechanisms recognising each other, like tuning forks
- Oscar's player was given the moment first, without narration moving past it
- Oscar chose to share everything with the party — they enter Session 6 with full context
- The level-up was called here, framed in the fiction as transformation, not a mechanical pause
- Vessel-Null's body was examined further, then left behind in the Hollow Nave
- The party climbed back out — the passage felt narrower going up, the dark less attentive

## The Witchlight Sighting

- Crossing one of Rootspire's outer platforms above the river after surfacing, **Zennor alone** saw a woman standing on the far bank — still, glowing faintly, a light that was neither firelight nor moonlight
- She looked directly at him. No gesture, no call out. He blinked, and she was gone — no splash, no footsteps, the river undisturbed
- Investigating the bank turned up no footprints except a single handprint pressed into the wet mud — flat, fingers spread, and dry at the center despite the surrounding mud
- The notice board's posting about a "woman cloaked in lantern-glow" had its contact name torn away — a person in Concord traveling clothes removed it two days earlier and paid the board-keeper to forget they'd been there
- This closed the session. The Listening Rock and the boot-print reveal were not reached — as far as the party knows, the letter's "three days out" still holds
