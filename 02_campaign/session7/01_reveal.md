## What This Scene Does

Session 6's actual play got almost all the way to the scripted cliffhanger and then the recording ended mid-scene — Maria had named the Echo, named Dawnswell, and the party had just shared a real harmony with her. What never happened on-screen was the specific, personal gut-punch: her recognizing *Oscar*, not just the concept of a Dawnswell construct. This scene exists to land that, cleanly, before the session moves anywhere else. Treat it as the last five minutes of last week, not new content — the table already has all the context they need.

---

## Must-Hit Beats

> [!important] Must Hit
> **① Maria's eyes go to Oscar mid-sentence.** She's talking about the Echo in the abstract, and something about being in the same room as Oscar for this long tips her from theory into recognition.

> [!important] Must Hit
> **② The Hidden Orders line lands, specifically pointed.** *"If the Echo cannot be stabilized, it must be severed."* — and for the first time, it's unclear whether she's ever fully meant Fergie by that.

> [!important] Must Hit
> **③ Give every player one line of reaction before moving to Scene 02.** This is the emotional peak of two sessions' worth of build. Don't rush past it into logistics.

---

## Running the Scene

Pick this up mid-conversation — no reset, no re-establishing shot. She's still holding the logbook, or reaching for it; the instruments are still warm from the second harmony.

> [!quote] Read Aloud — The Crack
> She's mid-sentence — something about the Echo being "a shape she recognizes without being able to name it" — when she stops.
>
> Not dramatically. Just stops, the way you stop walking when you've remembered something in the middle of a step.
>
> Her eyes have gone past Fergie. To Oscar.
>
> *"Dawnswell."*
>
> She says it like a word she's been carrying and didn't expect to need today.
>
> *"You're one of theirs. I was told to watch for you before I was told to watch for him."*

Let that sit — genuinely, silently — before continuing.

> [!quote] Read Aloud — The Order
> For a moment she looks less like a woman who invited them in for tea and more like someone repeating an instruction she was given and has never once said out loud.
>
> *"If the Echo cannot be stabilized, it must be severed."*
>
> A pause. Something almost like regret crosses her face.
>
> *"That's not about him."* — a glance at Fergie — *"That's about you. Or — it wasn't. Until now."*

---

## If the Party Pushes Immediately

> [!tip] Optional — keep every answer short; this scene isn't the place to resolve the mystery
> The table just heard this cold, with a full night's remove from Session 6 — expect at least one immediate follow-up.

**"Who gave you that order?"**
> *"Someone I was told to trust before I was told to think about whether that trust made sense. I don't know the name above the order. I'm not sure I'm meant to."*

**"Are you going to do it? Sever him — or Oscar?"**
> *(A long pause. She doesn't answer yes or no.)* *"I came here to watch. Nobody told me what to do once watching stopped being enough."*

**"What is Oscar to you people?"**
> *"I don't know. I know he's a* before. *Before whatever I am. That's all the word 'Dawnswell' has ever meant to me — that something came first, and didn't go the way they wanted."*

**If someone threatens her over it:**
> Her stone flares inverted-cold, bright enough to sting the eyes for a second. She looks as startled by it as anyone else in the room — it went off on its own. *"That won't make me know something I don't."*

---

## Reaction Prompts — One Line Each

Give every player a brief, genuine chance to react in character before moving on. Don't force it — a single line is plenty.

- **Fergie:** What does it mean to hear the order was never really about him?
- **Oscar:** First time hearing, from an outside, official voice, that they were being watched before this session even began.
- **Zennor:** Whatever his read on Maria has been so far, this is the moment it either sharpens into distrust or softens into recognizing someone else who's also being hunted by something bigger than her.
- **Hog:** However he chooses to react to a threat aimed at a party member — protective, wary, ready to act, or looking for the nearest thing to punch.

## Transitioning Out

> [!warning] DM Transition Note
> Don't let the scene sprawl into Scene 02's territory — the moment the reactions are given, cut. Maria needs a beat of silence before she's ready to answer the bigger question of what happens next.
>
> *"Nobody moves for a second. The blue flame in her hair is very still. Then, quietly, not quite meeting anyone's eyes:"*
>
> *"I told you to ask me again when I'd had a night."* *(A breath.)* *"I've had it."*

*Move to Scene 02.*
