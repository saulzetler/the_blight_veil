## What This Scene Does

A pressure-release scene between two heavy beats — Maria's reveal behind them, a fight and a trial ahead. Give the table room to talk, process, and just be a party moving through the world for half an hour before the next thing happens. Nothing here is mechanically load-bearing; it's all texture and optional character beats.

---

## Must-Hit Beats

> [!important] Must Hit
> **① The party covers real distance into unfamiliar Ashroot** — further from Rootspire than they've ever been, deeper than the Listening Rock, deeper than Maria's house.

> [!important] Must Hit
> **② Something about the ground or the light changes right before Scene 04 begins.** This is your cue into the ambush — don't let the patrol appear with no warning at all; let the world tip first.

## Running the Scene

> [!quote] Read Aloud — The Road East
> The path Maria's map shows isn't really a path — more a suggestion, worn faint by something that doesn't walk on two legs often enough to keep it clear. The Ashroot here is older than anything you've walked through. The trees don't just grow tall; they grow *patient*, the way things grow when nothing has interrupted them in a very long time.
>
> The light changes character as the hours pass — not dimmer, just older, the green going amber-grey the way late afternoon does before anyone's decided to call it evening.

Let the table set their own pace of conversation here. This scene works as well in five minutes as in twenty-five — read the room.

---

## Optional Beats

> [!tip] Optional — use one or two, not all
> - **Word of Timoor.** If the party asks around, or if Maria's still with them and thinks to mention it, a secondhand rumor surfaces: a man matching his description was seen three days ago, alive, heading roughly the direction the party came from — toward Rootspire, not away from it. Nothing more specific than that. Leave it open.
> - **A Cindersong marker.** A tally-scratch on a tree, identical in style to the tunnel from Session 6 — someone from that network has been through here too, recently. Doesn't need to connect to anything yet.
> - **Maria's Ashroot.** If she's along, she's quieter out here than in her own house — this is farther from home than she's been in longer than she'll say. A small, human beat: she flinches at a bird call, then laughs at herself for it.
> - **Fergie and the Porter Stone.** A double-pulse, warm-then-cold, same as the one from `EXT_heat_without_flame` and Session 6's camp — confirmation the Echo bleed hasn't stopped, it's just quieter now that its source is walking next to him (if Maria came) or behind him (if she didn't).
> - **Zennor's amulet.** Goes cold once, briefly, for no traceable reason, and warms back up before anyone can ask him about it.
> - **Hog's gauntlet token.** The storm sigil flares once, unprompted — same texture as Session 6's camp beat. Still no explanation offered.
