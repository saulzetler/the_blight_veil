## Before You Begin — Session Anchors

> [!warning] DM Only — Hold These in Mind All Session
>
> - **Session 6 ended mid-scene, not at the scripted cliffhanger.** The transcript of actual play landed most of the Dawnswell/Echo reveal but never delivered Maria's line recognizing Oscar specifically, and never cut to black. Scene 01 exists to finish that beat properly before moving on — treat it as unfinished business from last time, not new content.
> - **The goal for tonight: the party reaches a resonance site by the end of the session.** Everything is built to get there — pace accordingly. If you're behind schedule, cut from Scene 03 first (see Clock Checkpoints below), not from Scene 05 or 06.
> - **The combat in Scene 04 is meant to be short and real, not scripted-safe.** Unlike Session 6's "nobody dies today" rule, this is a genuine fight — two Concord soldiers, not a Pillar, not overwhelming, but with actual stakes. Let the party's choices (subdue vs. kill, stealth vs. confrontation) matter.
> - **Scene 05 is a skill challenge shaped like combat, not combat wearing a roleplay costume.** Roll initiative once, resolve in rounds, but every "attack" is an offering, a memory, or a truth. No HP, no damage dice — track successes and cracks instead. Full mechanic is in `05_threshold.md`.
> - **Don't resolve who — or what — is at the heart of the site.** Scene 06 ends on an image, not an answer. Session 6 taught this table that a cliffhanger works. Trust it again.

## Secrets & Clues Bank

> [!warning] DM Only — Unassigned On Purpose
> Ten true things the party could plausibly learn tonight, not tied to any specific scene. Spend one when a player does something clever, or the scene needs a reward. Unspent ones carry forward to Session 8 at no cost.

1. The Concord patrol in Scene 04 isn't hunting the party specifically — they're canvassing the whole eastern Ashroot on a standing order. Thane's search is wide, not yet narrow.
2. One of the two soldiers is barely out of training. If questioned rather than killed, they'll say more than they should.
3. Maria's logbook (if Zennor rereads it) has a date matching the Concord's last visit to the Listening Rock — she was there, weeks after Mira described the Vision scholars leaving.
4. The tally-mark tunnel from Session 6 and the Clenched Root are roughly the same distance from Rootspire, in almost opposite directions — as if something is ringing the city at a fixed radius.
5. The soldiers' gear includes a folded map with four sites circled — the same four on Maria's wall, plus a fifth she doesn't have marked, off the western edge of the paper.
6. The Clenched Root's "compression" is measurable: Arcana checks during the trial reveal the pulse interval is under a minute, not three — far faster than the Listening Rock.
7. Whatever is at the site's heart has been there long enough for root growth to partially enclose it — this did not happen recently.
8. Oscar's terrarium does not sync here the way it did at the Listening Rock. It goes still instead — the first time it's ever done that.
9. Fergie's Porter Stone and Maria's black stone (if she came) react to the site identically, in perfect unison, for the first time — previously they'd only ever mirrored each other.
10. Someone — not Concord, not Cindersong — has visited this site more recently than either. Fresh, non-military bootprints, days old, leading in and not out.
