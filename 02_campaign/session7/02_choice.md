## What This Scene Does

Session 6's `06_cliffhanger.md` flagged this explicitly: *"Come with us"* — *"Do not resolve this. It's Session 7's opening problem."* This is that problem, resolved. Unlike Fergie's kinship/suspicion/denial choice point, this branch belongs to Maria, not to the party — she's the one making the call, shaped by how she was treated in Scene 05 of last session and by the reveal in Scene 01 tonight.

---

## Must-Hit Beats

> [!important] Must Hit
> **① Maria answers without being pushed a second time.** She said she needed a night; she's had it. Don't let this become a second interrogation — she volunteers the answer.

> [!important] Must Hit
> **② Whichever branch plays, the destination gets set: a fourth site, "further east along the ridgeline."** This is the direction Mira Ashwick mentioned knowing about back in Session 5 but never visited herself.

> [!important] Must Hit
> **③ The choice should cost her something regardless of outcome.** This isn't a free menu — joining means leaving four hundred-plus days of routine and a house full of evidence; refusing means possibly never getting another chance like this one.

## Maria's Three Answers

Present this as her decision, not a menu you're handing the party — but let their tone in Scenes 01 and prior sessions (kinship, suspicion, denial, how Fergie treated her expertise) visibly weight which way she leans. If it's genuinely unclear how the table has played it, default to **Branch B**.

### Branch A — She Joins

> [!quote] Maria, if the party has earned trust
> *"I'm coming with you. I don't know if that's brave or just tired of watching from a window. Either one gets us to the same place."*

- Maria travels with the party for the rest of the session (and beyond, until a future scene changes it). She's a genuine ally in Scene 04's fight — treat her as a fifth combatant using the **Concord Purifier** stat block from `concord_patrol.md` reflavored as her own inverted-flame abilities (radiant → cold damage; otherwise unchanged), or simply narrate her as a support presence if you'd rather not track a fifth character sheet.
- In Scene 05's trial, she's the one who can tell the party *what the site wants* — see `05_threshold.md`'s note on her role there.
- Cost: she's now fully exposed. Her weekly filing habit stops the moment she leaves the house. The Concord will notice her silence eventually.

### Branch B — She Helps From a Distance

> [!quote] Maria, if the party's been respectful but she's not ready to leave
> *"I can't leave this house yet. Not because I don't trust you — because everything I have is here, and I don't know yet if I'm someone who's allowed to walk away from four hundred days of watching."*
>
> *(She presses the folded map into whoever's closest.)* *"This is everything I have on the fourth site. I've never been. Something about it has always felt — closed. Like it hasn't decided to let you in yet."*

- The party proceeds without her for Scenes 03–06. She stays behind, but the relationship remains warm and open for a future session.
- She gives them the map (matches the wall chart from Session 6) and, if Fergie asks for something to carry her with him, offers a small chip of her black stone — mechanically, a focus that lets a future scene reintroduce her voice/presence without her being physically present.

### Branch C — She Refuses

> [!quote] Maria, if Fergie was dismissive, or the party threatened her in Scene 01
> *"No."* *(Flat. Final.)* *"I spent tonight deciding that the safest thing I can do for both of us is stay exactly where I am, doing exactly what I've been doing."*
>
> *(Quieter, not unkind:)* *"You'll find the fourth site with or without me. Everyone always does, eventually. I just don't have to be the reason it finds you faster."*

- She still gives them general directions (the map, without her personal annotations) — she's not spiteful, just closed off.
- This branch is harder, not softer: the party proceeds with less specific information about what the site wants in Scene 05 (raise one DC by 2 across the board — see `05_threshold.md`).
- This door isn't locked forever. Note it and let a future session reopen it.

## Setting the Destination

Regardless of branch, the destination is the same: the fourth marked site on Maria's wall map, matching what Mira Ashwick described in Session 5 as *"one further east along the ridgeline"* — a site she knew of but never visited, because, in her words, *"it feels different. More — compressed, maybe. Like it's holding something it hasn't decided to let go of yet."*

> [!quote] Read Aloud — The Map
> The map is the same one Oscar photographed with their eyes back in Session 6 — four sites circled in the same hand, a fifth marked only with a question mark off to the side. The fourth circle sits along a ridgeline east of here, half a day's walk if the weather holds.
>
> Someone has written one word next to it, small, in a different ink than the rest: *held.*
