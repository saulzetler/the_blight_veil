# Session 7 — Music & Mood Cues
**Session 7 | The Blight Veil**

---

> [!tip] How to Use This Doc
> One track per moment, same rule as Session 6 — cue it, let it run, cut it rather than talk over a swell. Tonight has one real action cue (Scene 04) and everything else lives in quiet or tension; don't let the one combat track bleed into scenes that need to stay small.
>
> Nothing here is load-bearing. If you've got your own playlist, use it.
