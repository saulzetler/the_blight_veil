## Must-Hit Beats

> [!important] Must Hit
> **① Present the options as things the party already knows** — pulled from threads already seeded, not new information dropped in on the spot.

> [!important] Must Hit
> **② Make the Maria lead the clear standout.** It should feel like the most urgent, most personal option — not just one of several equally weighted choices.

> [!important] Must Hit
> **③ Whatever they pick, they won't reach it before dark.** This sets up Scene 04's camp naturally.

---

## Optional Beats

> [!tip] Optional — use if the scene has room
> - If **Kett's secret** hasn't surfaced yet, this is the last natural window — she (or word passed through someone else who escaped with the party) mentions a lone figure with strange fire, seen out past the city's edge.
> - **Briar Fenstep**, if he made it out with or near the party, has scouting knowledge of the surrounding paths and can supply the practical "which direction" detail without it feeling like exposition.
> - If `EXT_heat_without_flame` already ran earlier in the campaign, this is the moment for Fergie to connect it out loud — his Porter Stone's earlier silence near the cold-burn mark suddenly reads as more than coincidence.

---

## DM Prep Notes

> [!warning] DM Only
> - **Two or three options, never more.** More than that reads as aimless rather than open — the point is a real choice, not a menu.
> - **Voice the options through characters, not narration.** Let an NPC or a PC's own instinct surface each option rather than reading them out like a quest log.
> - **The Listening Rock option should feel real, not like a trap door.** It's a genuine, safer, quieter alternative — the party choosing Maria anyway should feel like their choice, not the only viable one.
> - If the party genuinely picks the Listening Rock instead of Maria: that's fine. Session 5's deferred Scene 05 material (`session5/05_resonance.md`) is ready to slot in as this session's back half instead — see the note at the bottom of this file.

## Running the Scene

> [!quote] Read Aloud — Catching Breath
> The shouting behind you fades into ordinary forest sound — wind, birds slowly returning to the canopy, the particular quiet of a place that hasn't noticed the city's crisis yet.
>
> Someone is going to have to say what happens next. No one is telling you this time.

Let the party talk. Then surface the options through whoever makes sense at your table:

### Option 1 — The Maria Lead (Intended Pick)

If Fergie's Porter Stone has already reacted this campaign (`EXT_heat_without_flame`, or the camp scene's beat if you're running this after Scene 04's foreshadowing instead — check your session's actual order), let him voice it himself:

> [!quote] Fergie, if he has context
> *"There's — I don't know how to explain it, but there's a direction. Like something's been calling and I only just noticed I've been listening."*

If nothing concrete exists yet at your table, let Kett or Briar carry it instead:

> [!quote] Kett or Briar
> *"There's a woman out past the eastern trees. Blue fire, cold as anything. I've seen the mark she leaves. Nobody talks about her because nobody wants her attention. But she's real, and she's close."*

### Option 2 — The Listening Rock

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> Two hours into the Ashroot, there's a clearing none of you have been back to since the letter arrived. Whatever's there has been waiting a long time. It isn't going anywhere.

This is the safer, quieter option — position it as available but less urgent than the Maria lead.

### Option 3 (Optional) — Just Get Distance

If your table wants a third option for texture, this is "we're not ready to commit yet" — vague, directionless, and gently discouraged by whichever NPC helped them escape:

> [!quote] NPC Voice
> *"Running blind doesn't buy you anything he can't undo in a day. If you're going to move, move toward something."*

## If / Then — Player Responses

| If the party... | Then... |
|---|---|
| Choose the Maria lead | Proceed to Scene 04 with this as the destination. Scene 05's approach description should reflect Scene 02's chosen exit route. |
| Choose the Listening Rock | Perfectly valid. Slot in `session5/05_resonance.md` (Mira Ashwick, the terrarium sync) as this session's back half instead of Scene 05/06 below — the Maria material can carry forward to a future session. |

| Try to do both in one session | Gently redirect — night is falling and they can't safely split the difference. One destination, one direction, tonight. |
