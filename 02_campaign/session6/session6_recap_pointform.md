# The Blight Veil — Session 6 Recap (Point Form)

**Party:** Zennor (Kyle) · Hog Thunderwood (Dawson) · Fergie (Paul) · Oscar / 05C4R (Lo)

---

## The Handprint

- Picks up from Session 5's close: Zennor fixated on a warm, child-sized handprint by the river, pointing into the water
- No other tracks nearby; no direction offered by touching it again
- Party reviews open threads: reach the Ashroot / investigate the four anomalies (main); unidentified construct, harmonic assessment team, Zennor's flame-resistant woman lead (side)
- Fergie's Porter Stone and Zennor's Jaihroquai (the Council's Anomaly Reliquary) both checked — no reaction to the handprint specifically

## The Sky Opens

- A Concord airship eclipses the canopy over Rootspire — first one anyone present has seen
- **High Marshal Varric Thane** appears at the rail: ceremonial steel, unpolished battle scars by design, flame-orange banner
- Public ultimatum: surrender those responsible for Captain Deyran's death and the defiance of the Council trial, or the city is treated as complicit
- Party is standing in the open market when it happens — impossible to hide
- A fireball lands ~20m from the market as a warning shot; shatters weak stalls, no deaths
- Thane, mocking: *"This is not the attack. This is merely the knock on the door."*
- City panics; initiative rolled once purely to sequence actions in the chaos

## Flight Into the Roots

- **Fergie** (18 Persuasion) rallies a chunk of the fleeing crowd to follow the party underground into the Deep Roots
- **Hog** and **Oscar** both try to herd Timoor (their tavern-met companion since Session 4) along with them; Oscar appeals on safety grounds
- **Zennor** critically fails a persuasion check trying to talk Timoor down — he flees the wrong way, loudly, turning part of the crowd hostile; his whereabouts afterward are unknown
- Second fireball lands near the retreating group; more of the crowd peels off, blaming the party for "bringing ruin" on the city
- Cornered in the tunnels by suspicious locals — party leans into intimidation instead of fighting: Zennor draws blades, Fergie sparks his hands, Hog partial-wildshapes into something toad-shaped (deliberately not lion — reads as less predatory)
- Three intimidation checks succeed (14/16/15); crowd backs off, concedes the Concord is the real enemy

## Briar Fenstep and the Cindersong

- A young scout emerges from hiding, offers to guide them out — gives his name as **Briar Fenstep**
- Opens a hidden branch/passage deeper into the tunnels (Hog's own attempt just snaps the branch)
- Reveals he learned this route from an associate connected to the **Cindersong** — same network that smuggled the party out of Orrenval in Session 1
- Party confirms they're part of "the Rebellion" too; Briar relaxes but won't name his contact
- Promises of secrecy exchanged both ways
- Confirms the Lower Eastern Roots (site of the rumored flame-resistant woman) are days away on foot — Rootspire's root system spans kilometres
- Briar excuses himself to check on his own people in the city; party lets him go

## Four Hundred and Fifty-Two Marks

- Tunnel beyond is well-lit, warm, faint old-smoke smell
- Walls carry hundreds of tally marks — counted at **452**
- Child's drawing at knee height: a tree, a sun, an unidentified shape above the tree canopy (not a bird)
- Zennor unsure if this connects to his family — timeline in his head is vague, "a multitude of years"
- Further on: a stocked rest area (seating, food, reservoir access) — used recently by someone unknown

## Out of the Roots

- Tunnel surfaces at a forest path outside Rootspire, early evening
- Oscar's compass confirms heading: east — matches one of Jaihroquai's four anomaly bearings
- ~3 hours walking (7–10pm) through open woodland; root-highway narrowing as they go
- Long rest called at the exhaustion threshold rather than pushing on

## Camp

- Hollow between root-hubs, small sheltered fire; Oscar takes watch, partial power-down only
- Luck roll: 13 — good rumor spreading through Rootspire that the party stood up to Thane and saved locals
- **Hunt:** Fergie sends Hog and Oscar for food; Oscar "negotiates" with a warthog it can't actually talk to; warthog charges, gets moonbeamed by Fergie and killed; Oscar carries it back cooked
- Hog harvests the tusk badly (takes half the skull with it), ties the head to his belt
- **Truth or Dare around the fire:**
  - Zennor: admits grief over Eris and Sela; toasts to being a new man, catches himself — it's for his daughter, not his wife
  - Hog: sent away young to monk-train instead of following his blacksmith father; complicated feelings about his family, hasn't been home since
  - A dare sends someone through an oversized pipe hit; heavy coughing, Oscar alarmed and unsure how to help

## Four Dreams

- **Fergie:** cold campfire, unseen presence studying him "like you'd study a reflection," a voice says *"I've been looking for you."* Wakes gripping a Porter Stone warmer than it's ever been.
- **Hog:** storm-cloud fighting pit, ancestor Thunderwoods in the stands, wins his fight the same chaotic way he always does (pants dropping included), a voice calls it *"not a flaw — a technique."* Wakes grinning, gauntlet token warm.
- **Oscar:** back in the Hollow Nave, Vessel-Null's crystalline housing turning to face them, lettering almost resolving, a pulse asks *"What were you made to be? And is that still what you are?"* Wakes clutching the terrarium, pulling harder than ever toward their heading.
- **Zennor:** a remembered kitchen, Eris and daughter Sela (working pink twine into a bracelet: *"Look at what I made"*), scene turns cold and dark mid-reach, the voice finishing the sentence isn't Eris's anymore. Wakes with the twine bracelet in a death grip and his amulet colder than ever.

## Morning and Maria

- Path shorter than expected; every attuned item reacted overnight (bracelet warm, amulet cold, terrarium pulling, Jaihroquai steady)
- Reach a fenced homestead; a woman resembling Fergie's bloodline but with cold blue flame instead of warm
- She speaks first: *"I've been looking for you."* — she had the same dream Fergie did
- Introduces herself as **Maria Zurita**, shepherd of the Temple of Radiant Dawn; invites the party in

## Inside the House

- Oscar names it directly: Maria was Concord-trained. She confirms, but pushes back on the idea that origin equals allegiance
- Read as Concord-trained, not Concord-loyal — closer to neutral
- Tea offered; several fake-drink out of caution; ale (her own brew) offered as an alternative
- House interior: harmonic instruments in deliberate order, a current logbook, a wall map of the Ashroot with the four known anomalies plus a fifth site marked only with a question mark (Oscar mentally photographs it)
- Maria admits to a personal quest tied to the anomalies, won't elaborate yet — party sets an in-fiction one-hour timer
- Group jam session on the harmonic instruments while waiting — pleasant, but something in the harmony doesn't quite land yet

## The Inverted Ember

- Fergie and Maria step away privately
- She reveals her own Porter Stone equivalent — a black stone showing a void; held together, the two stones let them see faintly through into each other
- She teaches Fergie **The Inverted Ember**: once per long rest, bonus action, 4d6 fire damage to a creature within 30 ft; self or an ally within 10 ft gains resistance to fire and cold until the start of their next turn

## Zennor's Search

- Zennor searches the house out of suspicion — 21 Investigation
- Finds sealed scrolls bearing the Concord Vision Branch symbol (matches the symbol from Pell's letter about the assessment team)
- Logbook: entries on the anomaly sites describe ambient wrongness only — denser air, misbehaving light, ringing headaches, occasional sickly/blighted animals — nothing describing an overt monster or danger
- Puts everything back before Maria returns; Oscar separately admits (unprompted) to having gone through her bedroom drawers too

## The Echo and Dawnswell

- The one-hour timer elapses; Maria opens up further:
  > *"There is an echo. And my goal is to stabilize it. But if the echo cannot be stabilized, it must be severed."*
- Describes it as a feeling more than a fact — a calling, a reflection, another presence not quite separate from her; Fergie admits he feels something faint too
- Asked about **Dawnswell**: Maria places it as a Vision-branch program, thought defunct, that built constructs like Oscar — she only knows the name secondhand
- Fergie offers the party's help; Maria accepts cautiously and thanks Oscar for a usable name
- Second, intentional harmony attempt lands — everyone gains a point of inspiration
- **Transcript ends here**, mid-scene — Zennor still in the logbook, Maria's story unfinished
