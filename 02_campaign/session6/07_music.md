# Session 6 — Music & Mood Cues
**Session 6 | The Blight Veil**

---

> [!tip] How to Use This Doc
> One track per moment, not a wall-to-wall score — this session lives or dies on quiet and silence as much as on sound. Cue a track, let it run under the scene, and don't chase it if the table's pace doesn't match. Cut it entirely rather than talk over a swell.
>
> Nothing here is load-bearing. If you've got your own playlist already, use it — this exists so you're not scrambling mid-scene, not to replace your ear.

> [!warning] DM Only — Protect the Silence
> Scene 06 is the one place this session actively wants *nothing* playing under the final line. If you've had music running through Scene 05, kill it before Maria says "Dawnswell" rather than fading it — the abruptness does work a swell can't.

## If You'd Rather Run Ambience Than Songs

> [!tip] Optional — for tables that find named tracks distracting
> Everything above assumes a table that doesn't mind recognizable music running under the scene. If yours does better with unobtrusive drone/ambience instead (Tabletop Audio, Wyrmwood Ambience, or similar generative/ambient sources), the same scene-by-scene emotional beats still apply — dread and scale for 01, anxious grey for 02, quiet decision for 03, four distinct dream-tones for 04, intimate-and-wrong for 05, flat unresolved drone into true silence for 06.
