## Must-Hit Beats

## DM Prep Notes

> [!warning] DM Only
> - **This is deliberately not a fixed script.** Draw the shape from `fergie_arc.md`'s Act II material and `maria_zurita.md`'s established voice, but let Fergie's player's choices actually steer the scene. Whatever they play, Maria responds in kind.
> - **She is Concord Vision branch** — quietly separate from the Might-branch chaos the party just fled. If a player mentions Thane's airship, let her reaction read as unease rather than solidarity; the Trifarix is not a unified front, and she knows it better than most.

> - **Oscar should feel something too, quietly, ahead of Scene 06's reveal.** Nothing overt — maybe the terrarium reacts faintly to her Porter Stone's harmonic signature, the way it reacted to the crystalline housing last session. A breadcrumb for Scene 06, not a spoiler.
> - Full stat blocks and mechanical detail for Maria live in `03_characters/npcs/maria_zurita.md` — this scene is not built to require combat, but if things go sideways at your table, her Acolyte-level block (CR 3) is the one to reach for, not the Echo-Awakened Seer (CR 7).

*(Full reference: `03_characters/npcs/maria_zurita.md` and `03_characters/players/fergie/fergie_arc.md`.)*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Race / Age** | Fire Genasi, Blue-Flame Variant · 18 |
| **Allegiance** | Concord — Vision Branch, Seer-Acolyte |
| **Appearance** | Moves like someone who has studied every step before taking it. Blue flame hair, ember-lit freckles pulsing with harmonic resonance. Concord Vision robes — layered geometric fabric, bronze filigree. A pale opal Porter Stone in her palm, humming in perfect inversion to Fergie's. |

| **Demeanour with everyone else** | Polite, reserved, slightly alien, overly formal — Concord training. Rare, startling moments of vulnerability break through. |
| **What she wants** | To understand whether she's real. To figure out whether Fergie is the danger, or she is. |
| **What she's hiding** | Her Hidden Orders: *"If the Echo cannot be stabilized, it must be severed."* Surfaces in Scene 06, not here. |

## First Sight of Maria

> [!quote] Read Aloud
> She moves like someone who has studied every step before taking it — precise, deliberate, threaded with quiet tension. Her hair burns in cold blue flame, illuminating sharp features and eyes the color of heated metal. In her palm rests a Porter Stone of pale opal, humming in perfect inversion to Fergie's.
>
> When she looks at him, it isn't recognition. It's recognition of *possibility.*
>
> *"I've been looking for you."*

Let this land, then let the table respond however they will.

## The Fergie Choice Point

*(From `fergie_arc.md` Act II — "How Fergie Treats Maria." This is the scene's real engine. Watch Fergie's player and let Maria respond in kind.)*

| If Fergie plays it with… | Maria… | Sample Line |
|---|---|---|
| **Kinship** | Softens. A bond starts forming, and she may begin doubting the Concord in front of the party. | *"I didn't think — I didn't let myself think anyone would look at me like that. Like I was the one who mattered, and not just the question I answer."* |
| **Suspicion** | Grows adversarial — controlled, but visibly hurt underneath it. | *"You think I'm the danger. Perhaps. I've thought the same about you since I first felt your Stone. That doesn't mean either of us is wrong to be afraid."* |
| **Denial** | Goes cold, convinced *he's* the unstable one, not her. | *"You can refuse to see it. That won't make it stop being true. I am not the one who's confused about what's real, Fergie."* |

Let the rest of the party engage normally — this doesn't have to be a two-hander. Maria stays polite and slightly formal with everyone but Fergie, with occasional, startling flashes of vulnerability if pressed with real kindness.

## If / Then — Other Party Members

| If... | Then... |
|---|---|
| Zennor is wary or hostile | Maria doesn't retaliate — she reads it, notes it, and becomes more careful with her words around him specifically. |
