## Must-Hit Beats

> [!important] Must Hit
> **① Maria's composure cracks when she looks past Fergie to Oscar** — she reveals she already knows about **Project Dawnswell** and recognizes Oscar's designation.

> [!important] Must Hit
> **② She lets slip her Hidden Orders line** — *"If the Echo cannot be stabilized, it must be severed"* — and, for a moment, it's unclear whether that order is about Fergie, or about Oscar.

> [!important] Must Hit
> **③ Cut to black before the confrontation resolves.** No combat, no clean ending. End the session here.

---

## DM Prep Notes

> [!warning] DM Only
> - **This ties Fergie's personal thread directly into Oscar's Session 5 discoveries** rather than inventing a new twist from nothing — Vessel-Null said "Dawnswell" last session; Maria says it now. Let a player make that connection out loud if they catch it. Don't explain it for them.
> - **Keep which Trifarix branch actually issued this order deliberately ambiguous.** Maria is Vision; Dawnswell is understood (by the DM, not the party) as a Guile initiative. Don't have her name a branch — let it read as "the Concord" in the vague, layered way a field asset who doesn't fully understand her own chain of command would experience it. This isn't a plot hole, it's texture: Guile is exactly the sort of faction that would use an unwitting Vision asset for cross-branch surveillance.
> - **Do not resolve anything.** Not whether she'll act on the order. Not whether it's about Fergie or Oscar or both. Not what happens next. The entire point is to end the session on a question, not an answer.
> - **This scene is short by design.** Don't pad it. Land the reveal, let the table react, end the session while the weight of it is still fresh.

## Running the Scene

Pick this up directly from wherever Scene 05 left off — mid-conversation, no reset.

> [!quote] Read Aloud — The Crack
> Something changes in her expression — small, but real. Her eyes move past Fergie. To Oscar.
>
> *"Dawnswell."*
>
> She says it like a word she's been carrying and didn't expect to need today. Her gaze doesn't leave Oscar.
>
> *"You're one of theirs. I was told to watch for you before I was told to watch for him."*

Let that sit for a beat — genuinely, silently, let the table process it — before continuing.

> [!quote] Read Aloud — The Order
> For a moment she looks less like a Concord acolyte and more like someone repeating an instruction she was given and has never said out loud until now.
>
> *"If the Echo cannot be stabilized, it must be severed."*
>
> A pause. Something almost like regret crosses her face.
>
> *"That's not about him."* — a glance at Fergie — *"That's about you. Or — it wasn't. Until now."*

## Reaction Prompts — One Line Each, If Time Allows

Give each player a brief, final chance to react in character before ending. Don't force it — a single line each is plenty.

- **Fergie:** What does it mean to hear the order was never really about him?
- **Oscar:** First time hearing, from an outside, official voice, that they were being watched before this session even began.
- **Zennor / Hog:** However they choose to react to a threat aimed at a party member — protective, wary, ready to act.

## If the Party Asks Before You Cut to Black

> [!tip] Optional — keep every answer short; the point is to deepen the question, not resolve it
> A table that's just heard this will almost always ask at least one follow-up. Have one line ready for each of the likely ones so you're not caught improvising the campaign's central mystery on the spot.

**"Who gave you that order?"**
> *"Someone I was told to trust before I was told to think about whether that trust made sense. I don't know the name above the order. I'm not sure I'm meant to."*

**"Are you going to do it? Sever him — or Oscar?"**
> *(A long pause. She doesn't answer yes or no.)* *"I came here to watch. Nobody told me what to do once watching stopped being enough."*

**"What is Oscar to you people?"**
> *"I don't know. I know he's a *before.* Before whatever I am. That's all the word 'Dawnswell' has ever meant to me — that something came first, and didn't go the way they wanted."*

**If Fergie asks her directly whether she still means what she said earlier (kinship/suspicion/denial callback):**
> *"Yes."* *(Just that. Whatever she said before, she doesn't take it back — even now.)*

**If someone threatens her to force an answer:**
> Her Stone flares inverted-cold, bright enough to sting the eyes for a second. She looks as startled by it as anyone else in the room — it went off on its own. *"That won't make me know something I don't."* This is the last thing she says before you cut to black.

## Ending the Session

Do not resolve what this means. Let it sit exactly where it lands — a Might-branch airship somewhere behind them, a Vision-branch acolyte who's just revealed she was hunting Oscar before she was hunting Fergie, and no clear read on which threat is closer.

> [!quote] Closing Line
> Nobody moves. Nobody speaks. The blue flame in her hair is very still.
>
> *End of Session 6.*

> [!warning] DM Only
> If the table wants to keep playing past this line — gently hold the boundary. This cliffhanger works because it's unresolved. Whatever they want to do next (fight, flee, ask more questions) is exactly what Session 7 is for.
